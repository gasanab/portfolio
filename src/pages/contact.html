Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

I offer a range of web development services including responsive website design, front-end and back-end development, e-commerce solutions, and website maintenance.

What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity. A basic website might take 2-3 weeks, while more complex projects could take 1-3 months. I'll provide a detailed timeline estimate after discussing your specific requirements.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes, I offer website maintenance services to keep your site secure, up-to-date, and running smoothly. We can discuss a maintenance package that suits your needs.

How do we get started on a project?

The process begins with an initial consultation to discuss your goals, requirements, and timeline. After that, I'll provide a proposal and quote. Once approved, we'll move into the design and development phases with regular updates and feedback sessions.
